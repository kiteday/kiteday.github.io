Screen Time permission

AppLimit needs Screen Time permission to detect when a daily limit is reached and, when enabled, block the selected app. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.

Limits and notifications

Each selected app has its own daily limit. Notifications and blocking are optional and can be changed by opening the saved limit in AppLimit.

Data

Your limits and Screen Time selection tokens are stored only on your device. Deleting AppLimit also deletes these settings, and they cannot be restored.
