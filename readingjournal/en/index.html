Reading Journal Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Support · 한국어

Reading Journal (the “service”) stores books and reading records in your account. This policy explains how its iOS app and website handle information.

Summary: You sign in with Google. Your shelf, private book notes, completion dates, and reading reflections are stored with Supabase and are visible only to you by default. The iOS app uses Google AdMob for ads.

1. Information and purposes

  • Sign-in: We use the account identifier, email address, and basic profile information provided by Google for authentication and account management. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Reading records: We store book titles, authors, ISBNs, shelf notes, completion dates, and reflections you save so you can access them across devices.
  • App operation: A sign-in session is stored on your device. Book cover and spine images are loaded from YES24 and may be cached on your device or browser.
  • Book search: Your search query is relayed through Cloudflare to the YES24 book search API. The relay uses your IP address for request limiting.

2. Storage and visibility

Your account and reading records are stored in Supabase Auth and its database. Shelf notes and reading reflections are not currently public to other users. Signing out clears the device session but does not erase server records. To request deletion of your account and records, contact us below. We may verify your identity and retain information only where required by law.

3. Third-party services

Google provides sign-in and AdMob ads; Supabase provides authentication and record storage; Cloudflare relays book searches; and YES24 provides book search results and images. Information needed for these functions may reach those services. See the privacy policies of Google, Supabase, and Cloudflare.

The iOS app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner ads. Google may process device or other identifiers, app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and troubleshooting. The information varies with device settings, region, and user choices. The app disables ad personalization and Publisher First-Party ID, and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Where required, Google's ad consent and privacy-options forms are provided.

4. Your choices and security

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your records, or account deletion by emailing us below. Reading records are protected by account-based access controls. If your device is lost, review your Google account security settings.

5. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our service or data practices change. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact hyemin086@naver.com.
