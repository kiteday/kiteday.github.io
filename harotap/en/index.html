HaroTap Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · 한국어

HaroTap (the “App”) is a touch-playable harmonica. This policy explains how the App and its advertising service handle information.

Summary: The App has no account system or proprietary server. Instrument audio is generated on the device. A Google AdMob banner appears at the bottom.

1. Information the developer does not collect

The developer does not collect or transmit names, email addresses, phone numbers, performances, or other directly identifying information to a proprietary server.

2. Instrument and audio

Harmonica audio is generated in real time on the device. The App does not request microphone access or record, store, or transmit performances.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display a banner ad. Google may process coarse location inferred from an IP address, device or other identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for ad delivery and measurement. Actual processing can vary by device settings, region, consent choices, and ad-serving mode.

The App configures Google Mobile Ads for non-personalized treatment, disables Publisher First-Party ID, and does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission. The developer does not retain advertising data on a proprietary server.

Where required, the App provides Google’s consent form and advertising privacy choices. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Retention and deletion

The developer stores no user data on a proprietary server. Deleting the App removes its local data. Google’s policies govern retention and deletion of advertising-related data.

5. Changes

This policy may change when the App or applicable laws change. Revisions and their effective date will be posted here.
